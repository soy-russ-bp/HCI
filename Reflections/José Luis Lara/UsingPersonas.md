# Using Personas
*José Luis Lara Rubio*

We mainly used our personas to learn what we needed to focus on when building our prototype. After we actually started paying attention to them (which was much later than I'd like to admit), we finally began to understand what our user wanted from the app.

In my opinion, the most important aspect we "discovered" is that our ideal user is motivated to learn soft skills primarily to improve their likelihood of professional success. Therefore, adding functionality that helps them move toward that goal is going to be critical to their experience with the app.

We believe this can be achieved by either helping them build a network of like minded people or by giving them a way to showcase their soft skills to potential employers.

Both of these functionalities were previously discussed but eventually discarded because they were considered "too difficult" to implement for a first version. However, <mark>after revisiting ideas with our persona in mind, we realized that it's precisely these kinds of features that will make users truly find value in our app.</mark>
