# AI Prompt
*José Luis Lara Rubio*

I asked the AI to design a set of usability testing tasks to evaluate learnability. The results were very interesting because most of the tasks it suggested were very generic (like having a user log in and complete a lesson) but it also had some ideas for learnability testing we didn't even consider. Specifically, it suggested a test where users are shown a screen from our prototype and ask users to explain clearly what they think each element does (similar to think aloud but focusing on the layout and presentation of the screens instead of the interaction with it)

It is significantly faster to use AI than to do everything by hand, designing our tasks took wa longer than the five minutes it took me to make the prompt.

AI can significantly reduce the time needed to design UX testing tasks, especially in earlier stages. However, its understanding of your users and app will always be limited by the information you give it and it runs the risk of focusing on the wrong thing. 

Possibly the smartest thing to do is to combine both approaches and get the speed benefits of AI without sacrificing quality.

## Prompt
You are a UX researcher. Please suggest a set of usability testing tasks focused on evaluating learnability in a soft skills learning app. The goal is to see how quickly and successfully users understand how to navigate the app, start a module, and complete a basic learning task. Tasks should be realistic. The type of testing can be supervised or unsupervised

AI Tool: Chat GPT
