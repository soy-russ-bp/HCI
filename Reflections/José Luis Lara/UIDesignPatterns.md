# UIDesignPatterns
*Jose Luis Lara Rubio*
From the list of design patterns described on the provided article [[1]](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/ui-design-patterns) i'd like to highlight the "Steps Left" design pattern as a way to improve our app. Since users move through a learning journey and lessons with a predetermined number of steps, showing them how many are left to complete them would help them stay motivated and understand their progress. This small change can make the experience feel way more manageable.

Another thing I'd like to mention is that design patterns pretty much solve the issues I have with design conventioins that I detailed on my [FreeTopic reflection](./FreeTopic.md) because <mark>they help solve common usability issues while still allowing for creativity in how we implement them.</mark> They're not meant to limit the design, they are a way to help improve the UX and give familiarity to the user, while we focus on making the experience feel unique and engaging.
