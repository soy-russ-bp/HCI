# Using Personas

At first, we treated our personas as checkpoints that had to be met or reflected in the requirements, and it worked at first. We had requirements, but they were very generic. The change came when we truly analyzed their frustrations; <mark>That's when we understood: our mistake was prioritizing what was easy over what was meaningful. </mark>

We learned that personas aren't inputs, but rather lenses for filtering what really matters. Now, every design decision starts with a question: "Will this help our persona with their frustrations?"

Technical difficulty is no longer an excuse. Because, in the end, <mark>complex features are what turn an app into a real transformation tool. </mark>
