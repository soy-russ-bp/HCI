# AI Tools

At the moment, I'm able to produce a low-quality UI using a usability framework, because I don't follow a mapping or traceability that reflects the use of usability in the final UI design. That said, using AI tools to produce UIs has been bad, due to the nature of AIs: <mark>
they are probabilistic systems trained with data from unverified sources.</mark>Applying this to produce UIs has given me generic results that don't approximate the needs of my users, even when I pass along my requirements documents, personas, and design guides; however, AI has been an easy tool to use. <mark>However, the AI-generated UI can be used as a basis for adapting it to our project</mark>, using a usability framework and also using traceability mapping to ensure that non-functional requirements are represented in the UI. The role of UX may not change much in the future; AI can be integrated in such a way that it serves as a tool, rather than a substitute for the entire role, since AI has so far proven to provide generic results, but they can serve as a starting point.
