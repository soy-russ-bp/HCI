# Personas 

Lene Nielsens's persona template emphasizes capturing the following needs: <mark>goals, motivations, and pain points (barriers that prevent them from achieving their goals)</mark>. In the context of our project, we used it with soft skills. <mark>One of the goals our users will have is to strengthen their soft skills. Their motivations may depend on the soft skill they want to strengthen</mark>: becoming more extroverted to be able to talk to more people, or becoming more organized to lead a better lifestyle. <mark>Among the pain points could be that the courses or workshops they've tried previously haven't worked for them, or that they find them difficult to take (if they're in-person).</mark>

We obtained this by creating online forms for students at our faculty, and the analysis we performed was to create scenarios and generate possible usage profiles for the product, trying to empathize with our user.

Now, assuming an introvert wants to strengthen their soft communication skills, this would have a <mark>medium impact on the product, more on the UX side.</mark> We don't want to overwhelm them with all the topics they'll need to cover to improve their communication, <mark>so we'd have to design the UX in such a way as not to overwhelm our new users when they first use our product.</mark>
