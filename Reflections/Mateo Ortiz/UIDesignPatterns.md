# UI Design Patterns

In our project (assuming we have a dense catalog of courses to offer), <mark> the "Progressive Disclosure" pattern can help us a lot in the organizational aspect, since we faced a dilemma: How to present complex soft skills content without overwhelming our users? </mark>

Taking our personas as an example, Sofia, with her busy schedule, needed to see only the essentials so as not to become overwhelmed. José, meticulous, needed to delve deeper at his own pace without feeling pressured.

Thus, the UI design would be as follows: a tiered structure that progressively reveals information. Each lesson begins with three immediately visible key points: the essential core that Sofia can understand without becoming overwhelmed, and a drill-down button that displays additional content that José would value. <mark> The great of this approach is how it can transform the experience into something less overwhelming for the user. </mark>

That said, I see UI patterns as not rigid in their implementation, but rather rules to follow that make better UI design possible. <mark> They allow us to solve common problems so we can focus on what makes our application unique. </mark>
