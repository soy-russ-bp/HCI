Using AI for generating usability testing tasks revealed both its strengths and limitations. AI tools like ChatGPT can rapidly create task sets that would take a team hours to brainstorm. For instance, the AI suggested asking users to explain what each screen element represents, which is similar to a “think-aloud” protocol but focused on perception of layout rather than interaction. This type of task uncovers misinterpretations of visual hierarchy or unclear iconography that may go unnoticed in standard usability testing.

However, AI-generated tasks often remain generic. For example, it suggested typical steps like logging in and completing a lesson, which do not address deeper issues of engagement or motivation. This demonstrates that while AI is extremely effective for speed and initial ideation, it cannot replace human understanding of context, user goals, or the emotional impact of interactions. Our approach combined AI’s rapid generation with human refinement, ensuring tasks were both practical and relevant. This experience emphasized that AI is a tool to accelerate work, but designers must still guide testing based on insights from personas and user research
