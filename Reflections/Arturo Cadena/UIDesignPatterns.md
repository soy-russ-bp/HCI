# UIDesignPatterns
I realized that my team hasn't thought about using an UI Design Pattern. After reading the article of UI Design Patterns, most of them look interesting, but in my opinion just a few of them would be relevant in terms of usability and for the goal of the project. At the time of writing this reflection we have a few interfaces with not many interesting interactions, but we do have, without knowing, a pattern implemented. And this pattern is in the choice selection interface.

<mark>The steps left is implemented in the lesson selection and indicate which lessons have been completed and which are still pending. Now in terms of usability, this pattern is relevant since it helps to give the user a clear view of the progress which is important in learning apps. Also reduces cognitive load because it avoids displaying all the information at once by having visual and sequential steps. Finally, it reinforces user orientation by avoiding the feeling of being lost by the user.</mark>
