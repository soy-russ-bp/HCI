# UsingPersonas

Using Personas for the prototype wasn’t easy and, in my opinion, considering our main users age is harder, because they basically know how to use and interact with any system. But that doesn’t mean it’s impossible, especially when they need a specific element or something important in the application, which was difficult to find. Nonetheless with some reading we could find interesting things.

<mark>Analyzing Personas, we found out that our users are more worried about the content and not in the prototype, but one thing that might be important to include in the UI according to Personas is the progress</mark>. In a learning app, progress is something very important because it somehow gives you satisfaction and an accurate sign of how much you've learnt so far, and our Personas is a perfect example of that. Therefore, an element or feature related to this in our user interface was a must. <mark>We implemented this by adding a progress bar in the lesson selection and in the same interface the path which has the lessons already finished is another way to present the progress.</mark> One gives you exactly your progress on all activities and the other is a kind of steps left that indicates which activities are left.

## Reference:
L. Nielsen. “Personas” Interaction Design Foundation - IxDF. https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/book/the-encyclopedia-of-human-computer-interaction-2nd-ed/personas (accessed Apr. 30, 2025).
