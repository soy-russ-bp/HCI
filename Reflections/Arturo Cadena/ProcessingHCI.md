## ProcessingHCI
**`Lean UX has many principles to follow to consider a process lean, but they can be divided into three key points: design thinking, agile software development and Lean Startup method`**. The first one is basically about approaching everything with design methods. Secondly, and probably the most popular of the three has the goal of reducing the time and deliver costumer value continuously. And the last one wants to minimize project risk through feedback loop.

**`After reading what the key points are, I would say that the team process is not lean at all`**. Probably we applied some design thinking, because we focused on the users and their needs and tried to think as designers to find solutions. But I’m pretty sure that some things were missing in the “design thinking” part and this happened because we didn’t consider using it and also other activities or methods that are part of this key point were not done. We didn’t do an agile development because we didn’t even consider following a methodology and reducing time wasn’t part of our goals. And feedback was not regular, so Lean Startup method can’t be said to be part of our process. **`So, the team process can’t be considered lean because we didn’t fulfill all the points and methods, even though there are a little of them in our project`**.

Probably after explaining the last point, probably we have to put too much effort in order to consider our process lean,**`but we just have to organize and plan the process better, especially in the feedback. Now this wouldn’t cost much, and we can get very good benefits from it.  The process just requires a little bit more of organization`**.
