# Personas
In the case of our project, we want to offer an app where our users can learn soft skills efficiently. **`The goal category is relevant to the product because they tell us the soft skills they want to improve and why they want to use them, and therefore these will help us to know the content of the activities, as well as the dynamics of these. In the case of the items, we get especially the usage goals and emotional goals, because according to our perception and the information of the toolkit, those are the ones more related to soft skills`**.

Collecting them was one of the easiest parts. In this case **`the team agreed to use a questionnaire so our potential users could take the time they want to think`**. Some questions were direct to get this information, like what soft skills would you like to improve, and in the others the team had to make an analysis to get some the goal information. **`Continuing with the analysis, there wasn’t a formal way to analyze instead we had a normal meeting to do so and synthesize the information but in my opinion the result was good enough for the next tasks. Maybe with a formal could have better or not because we haven’t mastered one yet (this was a reason to not use a formal method)`**.

There could be many ways in which Personas can influence the product. **`The most important it would be in the activities`**. One of Ad Astra main goals is to offer the people an effective and new way to teach them. Knowing goals and ways our users would practice soft skills directly impact and make us think how we are going to make activities to satisfy our user’s needs. **`Personas basically told us that the activities can't be just regular tests and there should be innovation like interactive simulations`**.
