The field of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) has significantly advanced in recent years and is now widely applied in product development. This progress has led to a variety of methods and techniques, shaping specialized roles and approaches. One of the most prominent roles in this evolution is UX, which has become an integral part of many development teams and workflows. However, it is crucial to clearly distinguish the purpose, tasks, methods, and techniques associated with each discipline. The following points help clarify these differences:  

- The relationship between HCI, Usability, and UX.  
- A key distinction between Usability and UX, illustrated with an example from your team’s project.  
- A concrete suggestion for an "UX feature" that is not yet implemented in your team's project. Provide a detailed explanation of how this feature connects to a UX attribute, along with a method to assess and measure its impact.  

Understanding the distinctions between Usability and UX is essential for designing **`effective and user-friendly products`**. While usability focuses on efficiency and ease of use, UX encompasses a broader experience that includes emotions, engagement, and satisfaction. By carefully integrating UX principles into product development, teams can create more intuitive and impactful user experiences.
