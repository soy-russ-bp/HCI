# AIprompt
In order to design tasks to collect data for the usability attribute learnability, I used ChatGPT <mark>and the details included in the prompt were the usability attribute, the app the team is designing, the context and the goal of the test.</mark>

It took me just few minutes to create the prompt and comparing to the time we used to design the set of tasks, there is a huge difference between both and no meetings or talks with an expert were needed for the prompt. <mark>Now considering this, using AI might be a huge advantage because time is very valuable and the information received can be more than enough for the attribute. Even though in most of the cases doing things that fast and trusting ChatGPT just for being a “smart AI” could be an issue, because how do we know that these tasks are correct for the learnability? The answer is that we should analyze or in some cases modify them and in this takes time. So just considering the time it takes designing the tasks, AI is way better, but making sure they are the correct ones is another thing, and the time can change a little or way too much. The AI results can be bad othat probably using it could not become the best option.</mark> 

In terms of strategy, <mark>I consider that the team should think and decide by themselves the tasks and use AI to improve flows or adapt them under certain contexts or terms, and to tell the AI this, the people working should have knowledge of usability and be aware of their purposes.</mark> I write that because some of the tasks AI gave me where so simple and wouldn’t contribute to evaluate the attribute due to the lack of context ChatGPT had of the project or usability is not it's best domain area. 

<mark>Now the conditions to use AI might be when we want some ideas, the team doesn’t have time, or we want something to base ourselves on. Any AI can give you as many ideas as you wish and they might improve when it knows more about what we should obtain.</mark> But for me there are no conditions to use just AI with zero human intervention. <mark>Finally, the conditions to design them manually are when the system is complex, very specific, the team has experience designing tasks or there are too many details, but in these conditions the AI can also be helpful. I think that using both is the best choice and the way of designing is going depend completely on the project.</mark>

<b>AI used: </b> ChatGPT

<b>Prompt:</b> I'm designing a usability test to evaluate the learnability of a softskill learning app. What specific tasks could users perform during the test to measure the system's learnability?
