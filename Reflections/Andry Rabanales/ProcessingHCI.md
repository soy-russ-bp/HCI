Lean UX integrates design thinking, agile methodology, and Lean Startup principles, emphasizing rapid iteration and user-centered learning. In our project, we initially lacked a prototype and structured feedback loops, which prevented us from truly applying Lean UX. We were making assumptions about user needs without validation, slowing down design decisions and risking misalignment with real goals.

Reflecting on this, I realize that a hybrid approach works best: start with traditional UX research to gain accurate insights, then apply Lean UX cycles to iteratively refine solutions. This allows small tests, rapid iterations, and direct user feedback to guide improvements. For example, testing interactive lesson sequences with Sofia allowed us to adjust pacing and engagement strategies, while José’s experience informed the design of motivational feedback loops. Lean UX ensures design is efficient, evidence-based, and directly responsive to user behavior
