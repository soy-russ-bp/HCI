# Personas
Personas are great because they help us understand users more deeply, especially their goals. Instead of just knowing users are students, personas tell us what they want to achieve. I looked closely at the goals for our personas, Sofia and José. Sofia wants to improve specific skills like communication and leadership to get better job opportunities, and she wants practical ways to learn and network. José wants to improve his skills, especially communication, mainly to feel less insecure in professional settings and work better in teams, and he prefers to learn at his own pace. These goals are really important for our project because they tell us exactly why students would use our soft skills app and what success looks like for them. I'm guessing our team found these goals through surveys asking about their challenges and hopes. Then, we discussed ideas together to write the main goals for each persona. <mark>The impact of these goals on our product is big. Because Sofia's goal is practical application, our app needs features like interactive exercises or role-playing.</mark> But for José's goal of comfort and confidence, we need features like self-paced learning options, clear progress tracking focused on personal growth, and maybe less pressure to interact socially. <mark>Understanding these different goals stops us from making a generic app and helps us design a more flexible and useful tool for different students.</mark>
