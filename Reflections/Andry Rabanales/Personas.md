Our team used questionnaires to collect information for creating personas. This gave us clear goals: Sofia wants to improve communication and leadership for career opportunities, while José wants to reduce insecurity and improve teamwork. During analysis, we realized that goals like these directly affect how we should design activities. For example, Sofia’s needs point toward interactive exercises and networking opportunities, while José’s goals suggest self-paced activities and simulations that build confidence. Personas showed us that activities cannot be generic tests; they must be innovative and engaging.
Design conventions are both helpful and limiting. On one hand, conventions make apps easy to use because people recognize familiar patterns. For example, a “Next” button should look like what users already expect. On the other hand, if every app looks and feels the same, the experience can be boring and unmemorable. For our project, I think the best approach is to follow conventions for clarity and navigation, while adding unique ideas in the exercises or progress features so that the app feels fresh and motivating.
