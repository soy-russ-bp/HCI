Design patterns are standardized solutions to recurring usability problems. In our learning app, we implemented the “Steps Left” pattern to indicate remaining lessons in a module. This small feature improved orientation, reduced cognitive load, and motivated users to continue learning. Initially, I was concerned that patterns might constrain creativity, but I realized they provide predictable structure, freeing designers to innovate in content and engagement.

For example, showing progress steps allows José to anticipate task completion and manage his pacing, while Sofia benefits from understanding module structure to plan learning strategically. Patterns also ensure consistency across modules, reinforcing learnability. This reflection taught me that design patterns are not restrictive—they are tools that provide familiarity and clarity, allowing creativity to focus on enhancing the learning experience rather than reinventing the basics
