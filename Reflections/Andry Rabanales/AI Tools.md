AI tools accelerate prototyping but cannot replace the depth of human understanding in UX design. We used ChatGPT to generate interface layouts and initial mockups. The tool produced responsive and well-structured designs in minutes, saving significant time. Yet it failed to account for critical usability elements, such as filtering lessons, categorizing modules, and providing contextual guidance. These omissions demonstrated a key limitation: AI lacks awareness of specific user motivations, expectations, and workflows, which are essential in learning apps.

Overreliance on AI can also limit growth, producing work that is fast but shallow. Effective use of AI requires integrating its outputs into a human-led process: reviewing, testing, and adapting designs based on real user feedback. In our case, combining AI-generated ideas with personas ensured that both Sofia and José’s specific needs—career skill development and confidence-building—were addressed. AI serves as a productivity enhancer, not a substitute for critical thinking, empathy, or iterative design
