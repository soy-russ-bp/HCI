

In our project, we focused too much on UI (designing systems, accessibility guidelines) without thinking enough about the experience we wanted to create. These choices matter, but they are secondary to the bigger UX goals.

UX and UI are different but connected. UI is the visible layer—the buttons, menus, and colors. UX is the emotional journey—how users feel before, during, and after using the app. In our project, we sometimes confused these two and focused more on UI elements, like defining design guidelines, rather than thinking about the emotions and confidence the app should create. A good UI supports usability, but real success depends on UX, because that is what makes Sofia or José actually want to return and continue learning.
